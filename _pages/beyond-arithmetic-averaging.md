  <section class="jm-section" aria-labelledby="baa-summary-title">
    <div class="jm-section-heading"><h2 id="baa-summary-title">Summary</h2></div>
    <p>
      When a wearable heart-rate router outperforms arithmetic averaging, that comparison alone cannot show whether segment-wise selection adds value beyond a stronger fixed estimator. This preprint presents a comparator-aware offline protocol comparing arithmetic averaging, fixed learned bilateral fusion, an audited lower-quantile router, a training-selected policy, and a label-informed oracle under participant-disjoint evaluation.
    </p>
    <p>
      The audited router improved on averaging, while fixed fusion achieved a slightly larger reduction. The training-selected policy outperformed the audited router in the primary comparison; its advantage over fixed fusion was post hoc and remains exploratory.
    </p>
