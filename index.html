Jaden Moon

Reliable multimodal learning under changing tasks and observations.

I study when machine learning systems should combine observations, surface predictions, or defer, and how to evaluate those decisions rigorously.

I am a Dartmouth CS undergraduate in the HealthX Lab with Prof. Andrew T. Campbell. In 2026, I conducted radiotherapy AI research at the Mayo Clinic Comprehensive Cancer Center with Wei Liu, Ph.D.
