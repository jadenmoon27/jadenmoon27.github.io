## Project

**LDASeg: Latent Diffusion for Ambiguous Medical Image Segmentation** was a collaborative course research project in Dartmouth's Deep Learning Robustness course (COSC 89.31/189.31).

The project studied probabilistic segmentation when multiple plausible annotations exist. I contributed evaluation experiments and analysis, with emphasis on comparing predictive diversity and segmentation quality under ambiguity.

## Status

This work received a **Best Class Project Citation**. It is listed as course research—not as a peer-reviewed publication—to keep the public record precise.
