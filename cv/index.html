Experience

  • 2025 - Present

    Hanover, New Hampshire

    James O. Freedman Presidential Scholar
    HealthX Lab, Dartmouth College
    Advisor: Prof. Andrew T. Campbell
    • ReliaGate (accepted at WellComp 2026): Developed a post-hoc reliability router combining confidence, signal quality and modality trust, cross-modal agreement, train-standardized atypicality, and train-fitted embedding geometry to surface or withhold unchanged wearable-stress predictions.
    • Evaluated ReliaGate across WESAD, UBFC-Phys, EmpaticaE4Stress, and PhysioNetE4 using subject-disjoint folds, fold-local selection, and paired held-out-subject intervals; on UBFC-Phys, relative to the learned no-geometry routing system, increased coverage by 10.1 percentage points and reduced accepted risk by 5.6 percentage points. WESAD analyses showed favorable pooled outcomes could coexist with higher median per-subject accepted risk.
    • ReliaGate++ (ongoing follow-on work): Extending static routing into validation-locked temporal emission control and auditing cue precision, false stress and reassurance, contradiction and flip rates, subject reach, and episode coverage.
    • INTERSPEECH 2026: Developed a leakage-safe intervention diagnostic that freezes trained experts and fusion parameters while shuffling quality scores across held-out examples to test decision-level reliance. Across StressID and CMU-MOSEI, 25 runs per dataset showed near-zero native performance changes, while positive controls confirmed sensitivity when reliability tracked correctness.
